Follow-up Result · Extends AW-FNO (ICCFD13 2026)

Gate routing discovers turbulence intermittency (JHTDB)

A follow-up experiment extending the ICCFD13 paper to a second, more intermittent turbulence regime — not part of the published paper. AW-FNO evaluated on super-resolution of JHTDB isotropic turbulence (isotropic4096, 128², 4×): the rich-gate model achieved the lowest error (rel-L² 0.0232 vs FNO's 0.0250 and WNO's 0.0247). The learned gate's wavelet weight (1−α) correlates spatially with velocity-gradient magnitude |∇u| at ρ ≈ +0.48 (up to +0.58 per sample), concentrating on high-strain vortex filaments — behaviour absent in statistically homogeneous forced-NS data.

Projects

AW-FNO: Gated Wavelet-Fourier Neural Operator

Gated dual-branch neural operator (Fourier + Wavelet) for turbulent flow super-resolution, outperforming standalone FNO/WNO baselines across four PDE benchmarks (1D Burgers, 2D Darcy, 2D Navier–Stokes, 4× HIT super-resolution). On HIT SR: mean PSNR 57.40 dB / SSIM 0.9991 vs FNO's 54.38 dB/0.9985 and WNO's 48.98 dB/0.9944. Presented at ICCFD13, Milan 2026 — see the JHTDB follow-up under Research.

PyTorch Neural Operators Wavelets Turbulence

Transolver: PDE Surrogate Benchmarking

Benchmarked Transolver (Wu et al., ICML 2024) against FNO on 2D Navier–Stokes turbulence: 0.40% rel-L² at 183K params vs FNO's 0.46% at 1.42M — comparable-or-better accuracy with ~8× fewer parameters. Validated spectral fidelity against the Kolmogorov k−5/3 energy cascade. Also built an unstructured-grid (VTU/VTP, PyVista) RANS pipeline, validated end-to-end on synthetic airfoil geometries.

PyTorch Fluid Dynamics FNO Transformers

PINSTT: Physics-Informed Neural Spatiotemporal Tubes

Full paper reproduction from scratch with formal safety guarantees for robot navigation (arXiv:2512.08248). Three-stage pipeline: PINN-based spatiotemporal tube learning, Lipschitz-based formal verification, and a log-barrier online controller for Temporal Reach-Avoid-Stay (T-RAS) tasks.

PyTorch PINNs Robotics Formal Verification

Wavelet-GINO: Multi-Resolution Geometry-Informed Operator

Replacing Fourier kernels with localized multi-resolution operators. A wavelet-based extension of Geometry-Informed Neural Operators (GINO), improving stability and accuracy on irregular geometries with localized solution structures.

PyTorch Neural Operators Wavelets Irregular Geometry

Helmholtz Solver: PINNs vs FDM

A head-to-head study revealing when PINNs beat classical numerics — and when they don't. Solved the Helmholtz equation (elliptic PDE) with both methods, systematically mapping error profiles, convergence rates, and compute cost against analytical solutions.
