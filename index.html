Research Associate, IISc Bangalore

SciML Engineer, ZenteiQ AiTech Innovations

I am a researcher in Scientific Machine Learning, working at the intersection of mathematics, physics, and deep learning. My work focuses on designing neural operators and physics-informed architectures that solve complex PDEs efficiently — from fluid dynamics and electromagnetics to safety-critical robot navigation. I have co-developed AW-FNO, a hybrid wavelet-Fourier neural operator for fluid flow super-resolution (accepted at ICCFD13 2026), contributed to the SciREX library, and built data pipelines for scientific foundation models at IISc Bangalore's ARE Lab under Prof. Sashikumaar Ganesan. I am broadly interested in the mathematical foundations of operator learning and its applications to real-world dynamical systems.

Projects

Transolver: PDE Surrogate Benchmarking

Physics-aware surrogate framework benchmarking Transolver against FNO on 2D Navier–Stokes turbulence and unstructured-grid RANS aerodynamics. Transolver achieved 0.40% relative L² error at 183K parameters vs FNO's 0.46% at 1.42M — 1.5× accuracy with 10× parameter efficiency.

PyTorch Fluid Dynamics FNO Transformers

Helmholtz Solver: PINNs vs FDM

Systematic comparison of Physics-Informed Neural Networks and Finite Difference Methods for solving the Helmholtz equation (elliptic PDE). Analyzed error profiles, convergence rates, and computational efficiency against analytical solutions.

PyTorch PINNs FDM NumPy

Vision Transformer from Scratch

Built a complete ViT pipeline from scratch in PyTorch for CIFAR-10 classification — patch embeddings, positional encodings, multi-head self-attention, and transformer encoder blocks with end-to-end training and evaluation.

PyTorch Vision Transformers CIFAR-10

PINSTT: Physics-Informed Neural Spatiotemporal Tubes

Reproduced arXiv:2512.08248 from scratch — learning time-varying safety tubes for robot navigation using PINNs with formal verification guarantees. Implemented a three-stage pipeline: PINN training, Lipschitz-based formal verification, and a log-barrier online controller for Temporal Reach-Avoid-Stay (T-RAS) tasks.

PyTorch PINNs Robotics Formal Verification

Image Generation with DDPM

Complete DDPM-based image generation pipeline on CIFAR-10 — forward noising, reverse denoising, U-Net noise prediction, training/sampling scripts, visualization, and Docker-based reproducible setup.
